## Where open-sight-chain sits

- Physical AI (F) with a ledger (1) or rewards (2): **0 of 10**. The three federated VLA papers (F8–F10, since Aug 2025) have neither.
- Papers with all three parts (C8, C9, E13, survey E10) are all **LLM / general-purpose**.
- Not found in any of the 62: federated learning (data stays on site) **and** low-communication GPU training (DiLoCo / DeMo) in one system; a reward of *rate × improvement with a halving rate*.
- Closest: Templar Gauntlet (E2), FLock (C9), Wu et al. (C8), MAGNET (E13), Co-VLA (F9).
